SUPERSTORE SALES ANALYSIS
============================================================

1. PROJECT INTRODUCTION
This project analyzes Superstore sales data using Python to identify sales trends, profitability, regional performance, and opportunities for business improvement.

2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES
- Clean and prepare the dataset.
- Calculate total sales, profit, and orders.
- Compare sales and profit across categories and regions.
- Analyze monthly sales trends and customer segments.
- Identify loss-making sub-categories.
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profit.

3. TOOLS AND LIBRARIES
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and pathlib.

4. DATA CLEANING
Original rows: 10,800
Cleaned rows: 9,994
Missing postal codes: 11
Rows without Order Date were removed. Date and numeric columns were converted to appropriate data types.

5. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
Total Sales: $2,297,200.86
Total Profit: $286,397.02
Total Orders: 5,009
Average Discount: 15.62%

6. KEY FINDINGS
Highest-sales category: Technology
Highest-profit category: Technology
Highest-sales region: West
Highest-profit region: West
Top sub-category by sales: Phones
Largest loss-making sub-category: Tables

7. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
- Investigate losses in Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies.
- Review high-discount transactions and pricing strategies.
- Study successful products in the Technology category.
- Investigate the drivers of strong performance in the West.
- Monitor monthly sales and regional profitability.

8. CONCLUSION
The analysis provides insights into sales performance, profitability, product categories, regions, and discount levels. These findings can help guide further investigation and data-driven business decisions.
